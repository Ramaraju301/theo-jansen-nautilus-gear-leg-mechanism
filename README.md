# theo-jansen-nautilus-gear-leg-mechanism
# Passive Legged Locomotion via Nautilus Gear-Induced Gait Dynamics

## Overview

This project investigates a passive quadrupedal walking mechanism based on a **Theo Jansen linkage** driven by **non-circular Nautilus gears**.

The non-circular gear provides variable transmission of torque and speed during the gait cycle, while the Theo Jansen linkage converts rotary motion into a walking trajectory.

## Objectives

* Design and analyze a Theo Jansen leg mechanism with non-circular gear transmission.
* Study the kinematic motion and foot trajectory.
* Analyze force transmission and ground reactions.
* Evaluate structural strength and fatigue behaviour of critical components.

## Analysis Performed

* Kinematic analysis
* Force and load-transfer analysis
* Ground-contact and friction analysis
* FEM analysis
* Gear stress analysis
* Fatigue analysis using the Modified Goodman criterion

## Key Results

* The mechanism generated a biologically inspired walking trajectory.
* Maximum toe deformation was approximately **0.92 mm**.
* Maximum gear tooth-root bending stress was approximately **4.06 MPa**.
* Maximum gear contact stress was approximately **17.4 MPa**.
* Gear fatigue factor of safety was approximately **6.22**.
* Linkage fatigue factor of safety was approximately **1.28**.
